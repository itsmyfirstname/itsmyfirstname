Hi there! I’m Matt Hays, a Software Engineer based in North Carolina. After serving in the US Marine Corps, I transitioned into tech, developing a strong foundation in DevOps and data engineering. 

I’m passionate about creating scalable solutions and automating workflows using AWS, Python, and Databricks. I enjoy optimizing processes, refactoring systems to reduce technical debt, and streamlining operations. My expertise includes building CI/CD pipelines and GitHub Actions that enhance deployment efficiency. 

Outside of work, I love exploring new technologies, whether it's tinkering with my homelab, diving into 3D printing, or learning about horology.

If you're interested in collaborating or just want to connect, feel free to reach out at hi@matthays.dev . Let's create something amazing together!
